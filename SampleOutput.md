The following graph is generated by analysis.py. It demonstrates the Text-to-Signal Conversion by transforming raw tweet sentiment into a numerical trend line with a calculated 95% confidence interval.

Green Line: Represents the aggregated trading signal (Weighted Sentiment).

Shaded Area: Represents the 95% Confidence Interval, indicating signal reliability.

Trend: Points above the 0-line indicate bullish sentiment, while points below indicate bearish pressure.
